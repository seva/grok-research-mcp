# Terms of Use

This project is intended for **personal research use only** by individuals who hold an active Grok subscription.

## Intent

This tool acts as a local programmatic layer between the user and their own authenticated Grok session — equivalent in purpose to a browser extension or keyboard macro. It does not:

- Bypass rate limits or subscription restrictions
- Access Grok on behalf of any account other than the authenticated user
- Scrape, collect, or redistribute Grok's output at scale
- Compete with or substitute for xAI's products or APIs

All queries are issued from the user's own account, at the user's own normal rate, under the user's own subscription.
